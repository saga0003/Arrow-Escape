## Full description
Arrow Escape is a clean, fast puzzle game built around one simple challenge: every arrow wants to leave the board, but only a clear path will let it escape.

Tap the right arrows in the right order, open new paths, protect your lives, and keep your streak alive as the boards grow larger and more challenging.

Features:
- Quick one-touch puzzle gameplay
- Procedurally generated levels
- Progressively harder boards
- Offline gameplay for the core puzzle
- Coins and level streaks
- No account required
- Lightweight and easy to play in short sessions

Can you clear the whole board without losing a life?

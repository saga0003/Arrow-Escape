# Privacy Policy — Arrow Escape

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Arrow Escape works without creating a user account. The game stores gameplay progress such as level, coins, lives and streak locally on the user's device.

## Information handled by the app

Arrow Escape itself does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or a developer-operated profile.

## Advertising

The app is designed to use Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for advertising. Advertising SDKs may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interaction information, diagnostics and other information needed to provide, measure, secure and personalize or contextualize ads, depending on user consent, region, device settings and Google's policies.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is integrated so consent or privacy choices can be requested where required.

## Local storage

Gameplay progress is stored on the device using Android local preferences. Android backup or device-transfer features may copy this local progress according to the user's Android and Google account settings.

## Children

The initial release is not specifically designed or marketed as a children's app. The final Google Play target-audience and advertising settings must accurately reflect the intended audience before publication.

## Data deletion

Because Arrow Escape does not maintain a developer-operated cloud account, users can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Data independently handled by third-party advertising services is subject to those providers' policies and controls.

## Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality, advertising configuration, analytics or third-party SDKs change.
